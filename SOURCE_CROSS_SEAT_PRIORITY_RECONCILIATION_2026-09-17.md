## 8. Small corrections to the earlier Voyage ICE table

The later cross-seat sequencing does not make the earlier table's technical assumptions automatically valid. Retain its prioritization intent while carrying these already established distinctions:

- **Search same-parent exclusion is conditional.** It is appropriate for a cross-document pair product when specified. It is not a blanket legal-search rule; several passages of one filing may be required evidence. Diversity or deduplication must match the actual reader objective.
- **Query-role inspection can precede reranker evaluation without proving a disjoint space.** The source consumer's current request and cache must be inspected; the ingest caller alone does not establish it. A reranker cannot recover missing candidates, but can improve ranking among those actually available. Do not turn that fact into an unconditional new blocker or claim that one role setting guarantees quality.
- **Fifty candidates is a proposed arm, not a fixed optimum.** Preserve the existing adapter, truthful identity/BGE/Voyage fallback observations, and candidate/token bounds. `provider != identity` still does not prove that Voyage ran.
- **Instruction evaluation belongs to C's evaluation, not an independently parked prerequisite after C is promoted.** A controlled baseline/instruction comparison can remain one later consumer experiment.
- **Embedding input expansion and reranker text expansion are different.** Changing embedding text generally creates a new vector-generation obligation; providing more permitted text to an existing reranker changes its request/coverage/cost, not automatically its stored embedding generation.
- **Unsupported actions are not synonymous with useless ideas.** Quantization, context models, GNNs, and optional materialization stay out of NOW. Retain them as separately scoped hypotheses rather than declaring their entire research category “theater.” Do not globally disable the authorized embedded suite merely because the current corpus repair skips GDS and the public family is VOID.

These are source-reconciliation corrections and scope rules, not new model/API verification or work initiated here.
